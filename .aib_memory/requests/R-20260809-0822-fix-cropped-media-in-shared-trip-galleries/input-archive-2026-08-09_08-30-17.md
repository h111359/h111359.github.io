### Goal

Improve every public trip gallery using the shared trip-gallery system so thumbnails show the complete image, captions follow the media orientation, and visitors can immediately recognize that media can be opened.

### Users and need

Public trip-gallery visitors need to understand each photograph from its thumbnail without important content being cropped. They also need an immediately visible indication that images and videos are interactive.

The current experience is especially problematic for long story cards, including the cited Kyustendil images, where the displayed crop may show only a small and unrepresentative portion of the photograph.

### Scope

- Apply the change globally to every current and future public gallery using `trips/shared/gallery.css` and `trips/shared/gallery.js`.
- Current consumers include `china/`, `trips/2026/`, and future sites copied from `trips/template/`.
- Apply the behavior consistently to images and video thumbnails.
- Preserve the complete source order and existing event data.

### Thumbnail presentation

- Every image and video thumbnail must show the complete media thumbnail without cropping.
- Empty background space is acceptable when the media proportions differ from the available frame.
- Media frames must remain bounded and top-aligned rather than stretching to match the full height of a long caption.
- Caption length must not change the image’s displayed proportions or cause a more severe crop.

### Orientation classification

Classify media from the loaded thumbnail’s intrinsic width and height:

- Portrait: width-to-height ratio below `0.9`.
- Square or nearly square: width-to-height ratio from `0.9` through `1.1`, inclusive.
- Landscape: width-to-height ratio above `1.1`.

For media cards containing descriptive text:

- Place portrait-media text to the right of the media when sufficient screen width is available.
- Place landscape-media text below the media.
- Place square and nearly-square-media text below the media.
- At the existing compact-layout breakpoint of `53.75rem` and below, place all text below its media to preserve readability.

For media cards without descriptive text:

- Derive the card shape and grid width from the media’s actual orientation.
- Do not use the current repeating position-based card-variant pattern.

### Interaction cues

- Display a persistent zoom icon on every image thumbnail.
- Display a clearly recognizable persistent video/play icon on every video thumbnail.
- Icons must remain visible without hover or keyboard focus.
- The complete media area must remain an interactive control that opens the existing lightbox.
- Preserve the existing accessible button labels, keyboard operation, focus behavior, and thumbnail-error fallback.

### Constraints

- Keep the implementation within the shared static gallery system.
- Do not introduce server-side code, a build system, a framework, or new runtime dependencies.
- Preserve existing Google Drive thumbnail and preview behavior.
- Preserve event routing, search, navigation, media visibility, rich-text captions, and source ordering.
- Maintain usability at the existing minimum viewport width of 320 CSS pixels.

### Out of scope

- Do not modify `trips/data_editor.html` or its previews.
- Do not edit individual event-data files to solve presentation problems.
- Do not change the content of captions or other trip data.
- Do not redesign or replace the existing lightbox.
- Do not change unrelated legacy galleries that do not consume the shared trip-gallery assets.

### Acceptance criteria

- The two cited Kyustendil images are completely visible in their gallery cards without cropping.
- All other shared-gallery image and video thumbnails use non-cropping presentation.
- Portrait media with text places that text on the right above `53.75rem`.
- Landscape, square, and nearly square media place their text below above `53.75rem`.
- All media captions appear below their media at `53.75rem` and narrower.
- Long captions do not stretch their media frame to the caption’s full height.
- Text-free cards use orientation-derived layouts rather than source-position-derived variants.
- Image thumbnails display a persistent zoom icon.
- Video thumbnails display a persistent play/video icon distinguishable from the image icon.
- Existing lightbox, keyboard, focus restoration, error handling, event navigation, and responsive behavior continue to work.
- The layout remains usable without horizontal overflow at 320 CSS pixels.
